Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This notice explains what personal data Dumpster Toast Games B.V. processes when you visit this website or email us, and what rights you have.

Who we are

Dumpster Toast Games B.V. is the controller for the processing described in this notice.

What this website does not do

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, embedded third-party content or forms. It does not load fonts or scripts from other servers.

Server logs

When you visit this website, our hosting provider receives technical data that every browser sends, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page you requested and your browser type. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The hosting provider keeps these logs for a limited period under its own retention policy. We do not use this data to identify you or to build profiles.

When you email us

If you email us, we process your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message, so that we can reply and handle your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, if you contact us about working together, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We delete correspondence when we no longer need it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, Dutch tax law requires us to keep business records for seven years).

Who receives your data

We do not sell your personal data or share it for marketing. Our hosting provider and our email provider process data on our behalf. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email contact@dumpstertoast.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
